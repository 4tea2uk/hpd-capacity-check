HPD Capacity Snapshot

Capacity Awareness

These results are intended to support your awareness of current functional capacity. On their own, they do not demonstrate that capacity has declined. Performance naturally varies from day to day and may be influenced by sleep, workload, emotion, hydration, illness and normal variation.

Your own experience remains the most important indicator. If you feel mentally fatigued, less focused, slower than usual or less able to sustain effort, consider taking a brief micro-recovery before continuing demanding work.

Suggested Micro-Recovery Options

If you feel fatigued or simply want a brief reset, choose one micro-recovery option that suits your situation:

  • Physiological sigh: take two inhales through the nose, followed by one long, slow exhale through the mouth. Repeat 1–3 times.
  • Slow breathing: breathe in gently for 4 seconds and out for 6 seconds for 1–2 minutes.
  • Brief mindful meditation: rest your attention on your breathing for 1–2 minutes, gently returning to it whenever your mind wanders.
  • Movement micro-recovery: stand, stretch or walk for 1–3 minutes.
  • Visual micro-recovery: look away from the screen and into the distance for 30–60 seconds.
  • Hydration: pause to drink some water.

You can repeat the Capacity Check afterwards to see whether your current functional performance has changed.

These checks support early awareness and micro-recovery. They do not diagnose fatigue, illness or any medical condition.
